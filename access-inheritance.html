Access management

Access Inheritance

How user access may extend across the customer hierarchy.

Access follows hierarchy and permissions

A user's account placement provides an organizational context for access. Permission rules determine whether that access can extend to lower levels.

What this means for access

↓

Access may extend downward

Users assigned higher in the hierarchy may gain access to lower hierarchy levels, depending on the applicable permission rules.

◎

Store Groups can narrow scope

An optional Store Group can limit a user's access to the Stores within that Store Group.

Hierarchy placement alone does not define every permission. Effective access depends on assigned permissions and scope.
