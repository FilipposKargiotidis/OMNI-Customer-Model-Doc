Access management

Access Inheritance

How user access may extend across the customer hierarchy.

Access Principle

Users assigned higher in the hierarchy may gain access to lower hierarchy levels depending on permission rules. An optional Group can scope access to the Stores within that Group.

Corporation→Company→Group (optional)→Store
Access inheritance depends on assigned permissions and scope; Group users can be limited to the Stores in their Group.
