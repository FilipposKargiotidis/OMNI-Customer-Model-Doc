Why This Matters

Merchants may have complex structures and different users with different responsibilities. The model provides a scalable way to represent that structure and manage access.

From complexity to clarity

A clear customer model helps merchants organize their business, connect services to the right level and give users access that fits their responsibilities.

01

Complex enterprise structures

Many entities can be difficult to navigate.

With the model: Corporations, Companies, optional Store Groups and Stores have clear places in one hierarchy.

02

Many companies and Stores

It can be hard to see where operations and services belong.

With the model: Store-level services sit with the Store, while the organization remains easy to understand.

03

Different user responsibilities

Users need different views and tasks across the business.

With the model: Users can be assigned to the account levels relevant to their work.

04

Access control challenges

Broad or unclear access can make administration harder.

With the model: Account structure and roles provide a clearer basis for assigning permissions.

05

Growing governance needs

A setup that works today may become harder to manage as a merchant grows.

With the model: A consistent structure can support additional Companies, Store Groups and Stores.

One structure, clearer scope

Corporation→ Company→ Store Group (optional)→ Store & services
The hierarchy organizes the merchant. Roles and permissions determine what each user can do within their assigned scope. Explore the customer hierarchy or learn about access inheritance.
