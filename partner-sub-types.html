Partner model

Partner Sub types

A Partner is an entity with which there is a Partner Agreement. Partners can have commercial, technical or administrative access to services associated with company Stores.

Partner relationships

  • Partner sub-types or groups define the Partner's relationship with company Stores.
  • Commercial access may include creating and viewing agreements; technical or administrative access depends on the Partner relationship.
  • A Partner can be associated with multiple Stores across different Companies.
  • The Partner can access the relevant Store services, but does not receive Company-level access through that relationship.
  • The Company remains the legal entity and the parent of its Stores.
A Partner relationship connects services; it does not make the Partner the parent of the associated Stores.
