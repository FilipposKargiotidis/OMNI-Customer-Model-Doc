Partner model

Partner Sub types

Subtypes describe different kinds of Partner relationships and can shape access to associated customer accounts. The subtype model is still taking shape: Executive is the only subtype currently created, while the others below are concepts.

How subtypes fit into Partner access

A Partner user gets access to Sales Portal when added to the Partner account. Access to associated Stores is separate and requires an additional access group or the Executive subtype.

Subtype catalogue

The cards distinguish what exists from ideas for how different Partner relationships could be described. Concept cards explain possible intent only; they do not define implemented permissions.

EXCurrently created
AVAILABLE SUBTYPE

Executive

The only Partner subtype currently created. Executive gives a Partner broad access to the payments and services of the Companies connected to it.

With this subtype, Partner users can manage those customers at both Company and Store level, including the relevant payment and service setup for Stores under the connected Companies.

Access scope: Applies to the Companies connected under the Executive Partner relationship and their Stores. It does not make the Partner a parent in the customer hierarchy.

PLConcept
POSSIBLE PARTNER ROLE

Platform

A platform Partner could have different levels of OMNI access, depending on agreements with Swedbank Pay and the merchant. The concept ranges from viewing merchant data to managing it, while keeping merchant agreements and settlements with Swedbank Pay outside the Partner's direct access.

INConcept
POSSIBLE PARTNER ROLE

Integration

An Integration Partner could work across Sales Portal and OMNI, with a focus on merchant agreements and transaction visibility. Compared with the Platform concept, its access would be narrower and would not include operational management.

TEConcept
POSSIBLE PARTNER ROLE

Technical

A Technical Partner could support the integration phase, including creating or viewing agreements and managing access tokens for online payments. The concept limits access to transactions and ends the Partner's involvement once integration is complete.

REConcept
POSSIBLE PARTNER ROLE

Referral

A Referral Partner could focus on introducing merchants and managing sales agreements. The concept excludes merchant data and transaction access, as well as technical support or operational management.

Platform, Integration, Technical and Referral are concepts only—not available subtypes or confirmed access rules. A Partner may have more than one subtype, though this is expected to be uncommon.

Subtype is not the whole permission model

Subtype

Describes the kind of Partner relationship. Only Executive has been created so far.

Access group

Can provide an additional route to Store access. The broader Partner Access setup is still being developed.

Do not infer exact permissions from a subtype name. Access depends on what is configured and enabled for the Partner.
