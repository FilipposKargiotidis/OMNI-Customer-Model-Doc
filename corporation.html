Corporation

The Corporation is the top level of the customer hierarchy. It represents an enterprise group or holding-company structure and provides an organizational level above one or more Companies.

Where Corporation fits

Use the Corporation level to represent the wider enterprise when a merchant has multiple Companies. Companies remain the operating entities beneath it; Stores and their services sit further down the hierarchy.

Corporation→Company→Store Group (optional)→Store

Users and access

A Corporation is an account where users can be added when needed. A user assigned at this level can be given access across the Corporation and its lower levels.

C
Corporation-level userPotential scope: the Corporation and its Companies, Store Groups and Stores

The user's assigned role and permissions determine what they can do at each accessible level.

What the Corporation level supports

Enterprise organization

Connects related Companies under a shared top-level customer structure.

Central governance

Provides a place to organize and oversee a multi-Company merchant structure.

Broader organizational context

Helps users understand how Companies relate to the wider enterprise. Access remains subject to assigned roles and permissions.

The Corporation organizes Companies; payment services and transaction activity are associated with Stores. View the full customer hierarchy.
