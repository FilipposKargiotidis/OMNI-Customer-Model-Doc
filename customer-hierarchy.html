Structure

Customer Hierarchy & Accounts

The customer hierarchy contains Corporation, Company and Store accounts, with an optional Group level between Company and Store. Each account represents an entity or organizational level and provides the context for managing customer relationships and data in Merchant Portal. Payment services and agreements are associated with Store accounts, where payment activity takes place.

External users are assigned to the account level or levels that match their responsibilities, with permissions appropriate to each level. For example, a large organisation may have administrators at corporation, company and store level.

Illustrative structure

A corporation can contain multiple companies. A company can organize stores directly or use an optional Group to organize stores by region, brand, department or business unit. A Partner may have service relationships with Stores but is not a parent in this hierarchy.
