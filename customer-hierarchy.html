Structure

Customer Hierarchy & Accounts

The customer hierarchy contains Corporation, Company and Store accounts, with an optional Store Group level between Company and Store. Each account represents an entity or organizational level and provides the context for managing customer relationships and data in Merchant Portal. Payment services and agreements are associated with Store accounts, where payment activity takes place.

External users are assigned to the account level or levels that match their responsibilities, with permissions appropriate to each level. For example, a large organisation may have administrators at corporation, company and store level.

Illustrative structure

A corporation can contain multiple companies. A company can organize stores directly or use an optional Store Group to organize stores by region, brand, department or business unit. A Partner may have service relationships with Stores but is not a parent in this hierarchy.

How customer accounts are created

Account creation depends on the account type. Standard account setup is handled by internal Owner OMNI users; Corporations and special access groups require Development-team setup.

STANDARD INTERNAL SETUP

Created by Owner OMNI users

Internal users with Owner OMNI access can create Partner accounts and Company parent accounts. Under these accounts, they can create Groups, Stores and Test Stores.

Owner OMNI user→Partner or Company→Groups, Stores, Test Stores

Group membership: A Group can be assigned Stores that were created under Company accounts. The Group organizes those Stores; it does not replace their Company parent.

SPECIALIST SETUP

Created by the Development team

Corporations and special access groups are set up by the Development team. This is used when a Partner, customer or service needs account capabilities or configuration outside the standard account-creation flows.

Corporations Special access groups
A Partner account is separate from the Corporation–Company–Store customer hierarchy. A special access group is also distinct from the optional Store Group account level. See Partner accounts, Store Groups and EcomCorp access groups.
