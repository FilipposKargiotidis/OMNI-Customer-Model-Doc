Company

The Company is the commercial and operational entity beneath a Corporation in the customer hierarchy. It is the agreement owner and receives payouts, and it organizes the Stores where payment activity takes place.

Where Company fits

A Company may organize its Stores directly or use optional Store Groups to arrange them by region, brand, department or business unit. Store Groups do not replace the Company as the legal entity or parent.

Corporation→Company→Store Group (optional)→Store

Users and access

A Company is an account where users can be added when needed. A user assigned at this level can be given access to that Company and the levels below it.

Company responsibilities

Commercial agreements

The Company is the agreement owner for its merchant setup.

Payouts

The Company receives payouts associated with its payment agreements.

Store organization

Can have one or many Stores directly beneath it, or organize Stores through optional Store Groups.

Operational scope

Provides a Company-level context for operational responsibilities and user access, according to assigned roles and permissions.
