Partner model

Partner accounts

A Partner is an entity that has a Partner Agreement with Swedbank Pay. Partners can work with one or more Companies, with access shaped by the Partner's assigned subtype and access groups.

How a Partner fits

A Partner relationship connects a Partner to the Companies it serves. It does not make the Partner a parent account in the merchant's Corporation–Company–Store hierarchy.

CUSTOMERS SERVED

One or more Companies

The Partner may be associated with multiple Companies.

The Company remains the legal entity and the parent of its Stores. A Partner's relationship grants only the access defined for that Partner; it does not automatically grant Company-level access.

Partner users and access areas

Partner users have a Self Account area and a separate Partner Access area for associated Companies and Stores. The Self Account experience is available today; the Partner Access model is still being developed.

Available now SELF ACCOUNT

Partner account and Sales Portal

Adding a user to the Partner account gives them access to Sales Portal. This is the Partner's own account area.

In development PARTNER ACCESS

Associated Companies and Stores

A separate area for working with associated customer accounts and Stores. This access model is not yet fully developed.

PARTNER-FACING OFFERING

Sales Portal

Sales Portal is Swedbank Pay's offering for Partners to create product agreements and distribute them to their customers.

Important: Sales Portal access does not mean every Partner can create or distribute agreements. Those capabilities depend on the Partner's enabled permissions and configuration.

Store access today: Partner account access gives Sales Portal, but does not by itself give access to associated Stores. Store access requires an additional access group or the Executive subtype, which is the only Partner subtype currently created.
Available now

Partner account access

Assigning a user to the Partner account provides access to Sales Portal.

One created; others concepts

Partner subtypes

Executive is the only subtype currently created. It gives broad access to connected Companies’ payments and services and supports management at Company and Store level. Other subtype ideas are concepts. Explore Partner subtypes.

Still being shaped

Access groups

An additional access group can be used for Store access. The broader Partner Access model is not yet fully developed. See the EcomCorp and ImsGroup pages.

Different Partners, different permissions

Commercial

Depending on the assigned subtype and permissions, this may include creating or viewing agreements.

Technical

May provide technical permissions for the Companies associated with the Partner.

Administrative

May provide payment-administration or other operational permissions when configured.

These are examples of access types, not a guarantee that every Partner has these capabilities. The actual access depends on the Partner's subtype, assigned access groups and permissions.
