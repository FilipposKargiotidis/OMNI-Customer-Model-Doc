Store Group

A Store Group is an optional organizational level between a Company and its Stores. It lets a Company arrange Stores into meaningful sets without creating another legal entity or changing the Company's ownership of the business.

Where Store Group fits

Use a Store Group when a Company needs another way to organize Stores—for example, by region, brand, department or business unit. If that extra structure is not needed, Stores can sit directly under the Company.

Corporation→Company→Store Group (optional)→Store

Users and access

A Store Group is an account where users can be added when needed. Store Group-level access can be scoped to the Stores organized within that Store Group.

SG
Store Group-level userPotential scope: this Store Group and its Stores

Store Group access does not automatically include other Store Groups or the whole Company. The user's assigned role and permissions determine what they can do.

Learn about access inheritance · Explore user roles

How Store Groups are used

Organize Stores

Collect Stores into logical units such as regions, brands, departments or business divisions.

Support scoped access

Store Group users and permissions can provide access to Stores in that Store Group, according to assigned roles.

Keep Company ownership clear

The Company remains the legal entity and parent; the Store Group is an organizational layer, not a separate legal entity.

Stay optional

Use Store Groups only when they add useful structure. A Company can also manage Stores directly.

A Store Group organizes Stores; it does not own payment services. Those remain associated with each Store.
