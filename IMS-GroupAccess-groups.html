Legacy acquiring access model

ImsGroup Access groups

ImsGroup is a grouping concept carried forward from the former Acquiring Merchant Portal. It brought together acquiring Access IDs associated with the same Company and organisation number, supporting access to acquiring Stores—with or without PSP services.

Why ImsGroups were used

A Company and organisation number could be associated with one or several Acquiring Access IDs in the previous portal. ImsGroup provided a way to group Stores connected to multiple IDs under the same customer context.

When was an ImsGroup needed?

Multiple Access IDs

ImsGroup created

When a Company and organisation number were linked to more than one Acquiring Access ID, an ImsGroup was created automatically to group the related Stores.

Access ID AAccess ID BAccess ID C
One Access ID

No ImsGroup required

When there was only one Acquiring Access ID for the Company and organisation number, acquiring Stores could be added directly under the customer. A separate ImsGroup was not needed.

Company→Acquiring Stores
The group represented a way to organize the acquiring access structure; it did not create another legal entity or replace the Company account.

Acquiring Stores, with or without PSP

ImsGroups supported both acquiring-only setups and Stores that combined acquiring with PSP services.

ACQUIRING

Acquiring-only Stores

Group Stores that used acquiring services without a PSP service.

ACQUIRING + PSP

Combined service Stores

For Stores with both acquiring and PSP services, the ImsGroup supported displaying data from both service contexts.

ImsGroup is distinct from the optional Store Group in the current customer hierarchy. A Store Group organizes Stores under a Company; ImsGroup relates to the legacy acquiring Access ID structure.
