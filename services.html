Store setup

Channels & Services

Each Store is assigned one channel: Online or In-store. Services are then associated with that Store according to its channel, agreements and configuration. A channel describes how the Store operates; it is not itself a payment service.

The two Store channels

Every Store has exactly one of these channels. A Store may have several compatible services within its channel.

Online

A Store that takes payments through an online customer journey, such as a website or digital checkout.

Possible services include Online PSP / Pay online and Pay by Link. Card acquiring, settlement reports and invoicing are also available for this channel.

In-store

A Store that takes payments at a physical point of sale.

Possible services include an in-store PSP and payment terminal, APMs through QR Code, static QR codes, and TMS. Card acquiring, settlement reports and invoicing are also available for this channel.

A Store has one channel, while its service setup can contain multiple services. For example, an Online Store may have both Online PSP / Pay online and card acquiring. A Store can also have card acquiring without a PSP when that matches its agreement setup.

Services associated with a Store

Services and agreements are associated with Stores. The color labels show which channel each service supports: Online, In-store, or both. Actual availability depends on the Store's agreements and configuration.

Online channel

Online

Online PSP / Pay online

Connects an Online Store's checkout to payment processing. Configured payment options may include card payments, APMs or QR Code.

Online

Pay by Link

Lets a business request or accept an online payment using a payment link. This service is for the Online channel.

In-store channel

In-store

In-store PSP / payment terminal

Supports payment processing at a physical point of sale through a configured payment terminal. The terminal may also present a transaction-specific QR code for the customer to scan with a phone.

In-store

TMS (Terminal Management System)

Provides capabilities for administering payment terminals associated with Stores. Depending on setup and access permissions, terminal administration may also be available from a Company account.

In-store

APM (Alternative Payment Methods)

Additional payment methods offered alongside or instead of card payment. A PAX terminal can display a transaction QR code; the customer scans it to open Pay online and choose an available method, such as Swish or invoicing.

In-store

Static QR code

A QR code on a sticker, restaurant table or menu opens a Pay online experience on the customer's phone. Depending on setup, they can select a payment method and complete an order and payment.

Available for both channels

Online + In-store

Card acquiring agreement

Provides the acquiring arrangement for card transactions. It can be associated with a Store with a PSP, or configured without a PSP where the agreement setup allows it.

Online + In-store

Settlement reports

Give the business reporting about payment settlement activity for the Store.

Online + In-store

Invoicing

Invoices may be available for configured PSP or acquiring services, depending on the Store's agreements.
