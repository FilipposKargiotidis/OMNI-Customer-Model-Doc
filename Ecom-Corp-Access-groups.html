Partner & Access Groups

EcomCorp Access groups

EcomCorp is an existing access-group model used to bring online Stores together across different Companies. It gives assigned Partner users a way to carry out transaction operations across those Stores, while access to reporting, invoices and user administration remains limited.

One operating group, multiple legal entities

EcomCorp groups Stores for operational access; it does not replace the Company accounts that own them in the current customer hierarchy.

USER ACCESS

Partner user

Added to the access group to operate transactions for its assigned Stores.

CURRENT CUSTOMER HIERARCHY

Online Stores

Each Store remains under its own Company account and legal entity.

A Company account remains the Store's parent even when it has no users assigned. EcomCorp is a separate grouping and access mechanism, not a substitute parent account.

Why this group exists

BEFORE · LEGACY STRUCTURE

Operational grouping followed the old Corporation concept

Online Stores could be grouped under the former Corporation concept even when they belonged to different legal entities. In some cases, the parent account belonged to a Partner, and Stores did not yet have their own Company parent accounts.

TODAY · CUSTOMER HIERARCHY

Every Store has its own Company parent

The current model places each Store under its own Company account, whether or not that Company has users. EcomCorp can still group selected online Stores across Companies so Partner users can work across them.

In current usage, around 90% of EcomCorp groups are used in Partner relationships; some are also used under Corporations and other setups.

What access does an EcomCorp group provide?

The access is useful for transaction operations across the grouped Stores, but it is not equivalent to full Company or Store account administration.

Available through the group

Transaction operations

Assigned Partner users can perform transaction operations for the Stores included in their EcomCorp group.

Limited / not available

Settlement reports

Settlement reports are not available to Partner users through EcomCorp group access.

Limited / not available

Invoices

Invoice access is not available to Partner users through EcomCorp group access.

Limited

User administration

Managing users for the underlying Stores is limited from the access group; it does not provide full Store-level user administration.

EcomCorp access is scoped to the Stores in the group. It does not by itself grant access to all data or administration capabilities on each Store's Company account.
