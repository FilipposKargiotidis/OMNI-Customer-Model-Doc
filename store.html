Store & Services

The Store is the lowest customer level and the operational point where payments happen, whether in a physical location or online. Payment products, service agreements and related operational services are associated with Store accounts—not with Corporation, Company or Group accounts.

Store characteristics

  • Represents a physical or digital store, such as an in-store or online channel.
  • Is where transactions take place.
  • Can be connected to the services and agreements needed for that store's payment operations.

Services associated with a Store

A Store has one channel—Online or In-store—and can have a combination of services that match its channel and business setup. Learn about channels and Store services.

Payment processing

  • Online PSP / Pay online
  • In-store PSP / payment terminal
  • Card acquiring agreement

Store operations

  • Pay by Link
  • Settlement reports
  • PSP invoices
The services available for a Store depend on its channel, agreements and configuration. This is an illustrative list, not a claim that every Store has every service.

Relationship to the customer hierarchy

Corporation, Company and optional Group accounts organize the business and provide user-access scope. Store accounts are where payment services are connected and transactions occur. A Group may organize access to multiple Stores, but it does not hold those Store-level services itself.

See the customer hierarchy or read about access inheritance.
