Store & Services

The Store is the operational endpoint of the customer hierarchy and the level where payment activity takes place. Every Store has one channel—Online or In-store—and can be associated with the services and agreements that fit its setup.

Where Store fits

A Store belongs to a Company, either directly or through an optional Store Group. The Store is where the chosen channel, payment services and operational activity come together.

Corporation→Company→Store Group (optional)→Store

Users and access

A Store is an account where users can be added when needed. Store-level access is scoped to that specific Store.

S
Store-level userPotential scope: this Store only

Access to other Stores or higher account levels is not automatically included. The user's assigned role and permissions determine what they can do within this Store.

Learn about access inheritance · Explore user roles

Store characteristics

One channel per Store

Each Store is assigned either the Online channel or the In-store channel.

Where payments happen

Represents a physical or digital point of sale where customer transactions take place.

Services at Store level

Payment services, agreements and related operational services are associated with the Store—not the Corporation, Company or Store Group.

Services associated with a Store

A Store has one channel—Online or In-store—and can have a combination of services that match its channel and business setup. Learn about channels and Store services.

Payment processing

  • Online PSP / Pay online
  • In-store PSP / payment terminal
  • Card acquiring agreement

Store operations

  • Pay by Link
  • Settlement reports
  • PSP invoices
The services available for a Store depend on its channel, agreements and configuration. This is an illustrative list, not a claim that every Store has every service.

Relationship to the customer hierarchy

Corporation, Company and optional Store Group accounts organize the business and provide user-access scope. Store accounts are where payment services are connected and transactions occur. A Store Group may organize access to multiple Stores, but it does not hold those Store-level services itself.
